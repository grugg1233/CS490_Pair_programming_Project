## Project Background

In the early 2000s, individuals globally would watch any content that was provided to them via their cable box. To access video on demand, one had to record television shows or movies. Subsequently, the recorded tape could be replayed to view the content. If one wished to see a movie, a trip to the theater was necessary. Should the film no longer be showing at the theater, it was essential to purchase or rent a tape or DVD from a rental establishment. A nominal fee was charged for renting any DVD for a limited number of days. A due date would be assigned, and exceeding this date would result in the item being classified as overdue. A penalty would be incurred for each day the return was delayed. Companies such as Blockbuster and Netflix achieved significant success in this industry. Netflix remains well-known today due to its ability to adapt to technological advancements and modify its business model. Conversely, Blockbuster was acquired by another company, and few people are familiar with it in the present day.

The Sakila Database is a sample database created by the MySQL team. Its primary purpose was to serve as a reference in books, tutorials, articles, and similar resources. We will utilize this database as the foundation for our project. The Sakila database contains the necessary tables to operate a DVD rental store. Your task is to transition this store to an online platform. You will develop a front-end system that enables store clerks to manage their customers, inventory, and rentals. The back-end system will interface with the Sakila MySQL database and provide endpoints to support the front end.
